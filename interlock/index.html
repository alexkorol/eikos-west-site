Common questions

Answers before your appointment.

Will an interlock device damage my car?

Professional installation is intended to integrate the required device correctly with your vehicle. We will explain the installation and removal process for your specific vehicle.

Can you install a device in my specific vehicle?

Call with your year, make, and model so the team can confirm device compatibility and scheduling before your appointment.

How often do I need to come back?

Interlock devices generally require monthly calibrations. Your provider and requirements determine the exact schedule, which we will help you understand.

What should I bring to installation?

Bring required court or DOL documentation, your provider information if already assigned, identification, and the vehicle needing installation. Call first if you are uncertain.

Will my visit be confidential?

We handle ignition interlock service discreetly and professionally. Our team treats the purpose of your appointment with respect.
